Privacy Policy for Muslim

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Muslim Platform ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application (the "Application"), developed by Mouhcine Fadoul.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Precise Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Purpose: The Application requires access to your device's geographical location solely to accurately calculate Islamic prayer times, Qibla direction, and local Hijri date adjustments based on your current city.

Storage: Location data is processed locally on your device or transmitted securely to calculate coordinates. We do not store, track, or sell your personal location history on external servers.

B. Do Not Disturb / Notification Policy Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY)

Purpose: The Application includes a "Smart Auto-Silent Mode" feature that automatically mutes your device during prayer times (Iqamah windows) to prevent interruptions inside mosques.

Scope: This permission is strictly used to toggle the device’s Do Not Disturb / Silent ringer mode on or off during prayer intervals and restore your original sound settings immediately after. No notification content, user messages, or personal communications are read, stored, or accessed.

C. Notification Permissions (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Purpose: Used exclusively to deliver timely Adhan audio alerts, pre-prayer reminders, and post-prayer status updates to the user.

2. Data Retention and Security

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal user identity data, financial records, or communication logs. All application preferences (such as selected calculation methods, silent mode toggles, and UI themes) are saved locally on your device using encrypted local storage parameters.
